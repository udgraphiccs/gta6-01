Port Vela is an original open-world crime game built from scratch for the
make.fun gta6-01 bounty. It is a top-down 2D game that runs entirely in the
browser — no install, no build step, no dependencies.

## The game

Port Vela is a sun-soaked coastal city. You are free to explore it on foot
or by car: streets full of traffic and pedestrians, a harbour, back alleys
and open plazas. Cars can be stolen — walk up to one and take it. The police
respond to what you do, so a wanted level rises as you cause trouble and
falls when you lie low and stay out of sight.

There is a story to follow as well as a city to mess around in: a short
series of missions that takes you from small jobs to bigger scores, with
combat along the way. The HUD keeps you oriented with your health, wanted
level, cash and objectives, plus a minimap showing the streets around you
and where you need to go next.

## Play it locally

You need Node 22. From this directory, start the dev server:

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser. To use a different port,
pass `--port`, for example `node serve.ts --port 9000`.

## Controls

WASD moves you on foot and drives the car once you are behind the wheel.
Press E to enter a car and E again to get out of it. Space is the
handbrake for sharp turns and quick stops, and Shift lets you sprint while
on foot. Aim with the mouse and click to shoot. Esc pauses the game, and M
mutes the sound.
